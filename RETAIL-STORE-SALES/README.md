## 📋 Project Overview
End-to-end retail sales analysis using **Tableau** and **SQL**.  
Analyzed over 11,000 transactions to understand revenue trends, customer behavior, and product performance.

### Key Insights

- **Strong Average Order Value (AOV):** The store maintains a healthy AOV of **$129.65**, indicating customers consistently spend well over $100 per transaction. This reflects effective upselling and a strong product mix.

- **Overall Performance:** Over the analyzed period, the store generated **$1,552,071** in total revenue across **11,971 transactions** from **25 customers**.

- **Category vs. Item Leadership:**
  - **Butchers** is the top-performing category by total spend (**$208,118**), showing strong demand for fresh protein products.
  - The highest-revenue individual item is **Item_25_FUR** (Furniture category) at **$25,625**, highlighting the impact of high-value furniture items.
  - In terms of purchase frequency, **Item_2_BEV** (Beverages) leads with **128 units sold**, confirming beverages as a high-volume everyday category.

- **Seasonality Trends:** January recorded the highest monthly revenue (**~$174,421**), while October was the lowest (**~$119,413**). This clear seasonal pattern should guide inventory planning, promotions, and resource allocation.

- **Weekly Patterns:** **Sunday** is the busiest day with **1,736 transactions**, outperforming other days. Retail operations may benefit from enhanced weekend staffing and targeted Sunday promotions.

- **Additional Highlights:**
  - Furniture and Butchers categories dominate the top revenue-generating items, making them key growth drivers.
  - Electric Household items rank a close second in category performance (**$203,814**), contributing to a well-diversified revenue base.
  - Monthly revenue remains relatively stable throughout the year with moderate fluctuations.
